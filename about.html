ABOUT THE MOD

Deltarune No More is a mod that removes all LGBTQ+ elements from the game by replacing pronouns and changing scene,the mod is as faithful as it can be to the original story of the game even without those elements and the mod is completely optional to download btw and completely free to download

Disclaimer: This mod is not made out of pure homophobia; it is designed for religious players who want to play Deltarune without sinning.
